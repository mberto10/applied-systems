# Surprise slot

Read only when the contract's `serendipity` is enabled with a positive `max_items`.

Reserve its configured share of the existing discovery sample, within its authorized sources and limits. After regular selection, exclude listed interests and exclusions, apply every `serendipity.test`, and prefer topics absent from history. Use `slot: "serendipity"` and a short `topic`. None is fine; this never changes interests.
